## The idea

A Pokémon site where you can catch, view, compare and battle Pokémon.

## Features

- **Catch and collect:** catch Pokémon and see your own collection.
- **Current Pokémon:** pick one as your current Pokémon, which helps you catch others.
- **Compare and battle** Pokémon against each other.
- **Quiz mini-game:** answer correctly to upgrade your current Pokémon's attack or defense.

## Under the hood

- Server-rendered pages with EJS and CSS on Node.js and Express.
- RESTful API endpoints for reading and updating data, stored in MongoDB.
- Unit and integration tests with Jest and Selenium.
