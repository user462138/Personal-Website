## The problem

Microtron's sales team sent quotes straight out of their ERP system (Dave). They were accurate, but plain, and did little to engage customers.

## What I built

QUOTE+ is an internal application that takes those ERP quotes and enriches them with content customers actually want to read.

- **Customizable quotes:** templates, images, videos and layout adjustments.
- **Approval flows** before a quote goes out.
- **Single sign-on and roles** through Azure AD, with permissions synced from the ERP system.
- **Secure and responsive:** works on desktop and mobile, and is GDPR-compliant.
